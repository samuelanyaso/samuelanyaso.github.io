I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at the Arnold School of Public Health, University of South Carolina.

My research focuses on developing statistical and computational methods for high-dimensional biomedical data, with applications in cancer epidemiology and precision health. My primary interests include integrative multi-omics analysis, microbiome research, graphical and network models, spatial and genomic data analysis, cluster-correlated data, and time-to-event methods.
